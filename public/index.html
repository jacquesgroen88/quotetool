🆕 What's New

Recent updates to the Quote Generator

Latest
🔒 Supplier price check
Every quote is now automatically checked against the supplier document. A Supplier Price Check card appears in the editor — green when all prices match the supplier, amber if an option's price isn't found in the supplier doc or the per-person × pax doesn't add up to the total. It's a safety net to catch a mistyped price before it reaches a client — it never changes your prices.
↩ Undo AI changes
After any AI edit, an ↩ Undo Last Change button appears below the chat input. Click it to revert — supports up to 10 steps back.
📧 Quote acceptance email
When a client clicks Select This Package and submits their details, you automatically receive an email with the resort, price, dates, and the client's contact info.
💳 Price markup tool
Enter a percentage in the Price Markup panel to apply a markup to all option prices at once. The quote auto-saves after applying.
📱 WhatsApp send button
The Send via WhatsApp button appears in the link card once a quote is saved — opens a WhatsApp message with the client link pre-filled.
💼 Admin dashboard improvements
Search, filter by date, sort by name or newest. Client phone/email stored and viewable via Details ▾. Export all quotes to CSV. Duplicate any quote with one click.
